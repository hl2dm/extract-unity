## Art Direction Review — v0.8.1 (rv2)

**SOFTNESS_COMFORT — 8/10**
The light genuinely reads calmer now. `a_poly_slide`/`a_poly_house`/`a_light_bedroom` all show the shift from saturated coral/pink to warm cream-tan, which does as much for "softness" as the shader changes — harsh pink roofs read hot, cream roofs read gentle. The purple contact-shadow blob under the streetlamp in `facade_crop_b_vs_a.jpg` (left) is now a neutral grey blob (right) — correct direction. One miss: the cake's contact shadow in `b_light_bedroom.png` vs `a_light_bedroom.png` is an identical hard-edged, near-opaque black ellipse in *both* builds — the "soft radial alpha @0.30" treatment doesn't seem to have reached this particular shadow-map shadow, so it still looks like a flat decal next to all the new soft blob shadows elsewhere.

**DETAIL_RICHNESS — 7/10**
Where it's visible, it's genuinely good — `a_poly_house.png`'s foreground building now has louvred shutters, quoins, a proper window surround, and the cornice/fascia break-up instead of the flat pink box in `b_poly_house.png`. The problem is the new trim appears to be eating signage at normal play-camera distance: in `a_light_street.png` vs `b_light_street.png` (identical frame — same timer 3:54, same 29秒, same NPC/scooter positions), the right building's "冰店" sign is simply gone, replaced by a vent medallion, and the left building's "豆花" sign is gone, replaced by an address plate "34". That's goal 6 regressing, not holding — the dedicated `sign_flower0_after.png`/`sign_ice0_after.png` promo shots prove the signs *can* render cleanly, so this looks like an angle-dependent occlusion from the new pipes/vents/cornice geometry rather than a removed asset. Also, detail density drops off fast at distance — in `a_poly_slide.png` the background row of shops is basically a palette swap of `b_poly_slide.png`, none of the dentil/shutter detail from the hero shots survives at that LOD.

**OVERALL — 7/10**
Pink cleanup, contact shadows, and the flicker numbers (0.417→0.005 timer, 0.263→0.010 ring) are a real, well-executed pass. It's held back from a higher score by one functional regression (signage occlusion at normal viewing distance) and one inconsistency (cake shadow didn't get the soft treatment).

**Top fixes**
1. Fix sign occlusion on street-level facades — pull back/reposition the new vents, pipes, and cornice brackets so they don't sit directly over sign decals at the default walking camera angle (`a_light_street.png`).
2. Apply the soft radial-alpha shadow treatment to item/prop shadows indoors too — the cake shadow is still a hard black disc in both `b_light_bedroom.png` and `a_light_bedroom.png`.
3. `v081_calm_compare.png` still shows the old pink pill in *every* row (both labelled v0.8 and calm) — regenerate this QA asset against the current brown-pill build so it isn't cited as evidence against a stale screenshot.
4. Extend the cornice/shutter/quoin detail pass to background-row buildings, not just hero/foreground facades, so richness doesn't fall off a cliff past ~15m.

SOFT: 8/10
DETAIL: 7/10
