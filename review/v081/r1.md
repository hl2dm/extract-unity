## Art Review — v0.8.1 vs v0.8

**SOFTNESS_COMFORT — 6/10**
The pink→cream/wood recolor genuinely warms the palette (street buildings, bedroom curtains, living-room sofa all moved off hot pink into cream/tan — good, goal 2 landed). But the actual *lighting* hasn't gotten softer: the bedroom/living-room sunbeam shafts on the floor are still crisp, hard-edged cel shapes identical in b/a; specular rim-highlights on curtain edges, the cake prop, and lamp-post caps are still blown-out white hotspots with zero falloff. Contact shadows (goal 3) look unchanged too — the shadow under the cake slice in `a_light_bedroom` is the same flat solid black ellipse as in `b_light_bedroom`, no soft penumbra/gradient added. So this reads as a *color temperature* pass, not a *lighting softness* pass — won't fully answer the user's "光能不能柔一點" ask.

**DETAIL_RICHNESS — 4/10**
`a_poly_house` vs `b_poly_house` shows the same massing: rounded dome roofs and flat circular "flower/button" medallions standing in for windows. Some wood-plank shutter trim got added (nice touch, adds texture), but there's still no real window frame/sill, no cornice line, no support brackets under the overhangs — goal 4 is largely unmet. The geometry is the goal's core ask and it wasn't touched; only materials/props were refreshed.

**OVERALL — 5/10**
Signs (goal 6) are a clear win — `sign_flower0_after` / `sign_ice0_after` show 花店/冰店 fully exposed and legible, not boxed under the balcony shadow anymore. But `v081_calm_compare.png` is a red flag: one of the two HUD rows shows the "媽媽叫你回家" timer pill popping its pink background on and off every other frame while the other row holds steady — i.e. the flicker bug (goal 5) may still be present in whichever build that row represents. That needs engineering confirmation before this ships, since it's an explicit v0.8.1 goal and the screenshot evidence contradicts it.

**Top fixes**
1. Confirm and kill the timer-pill flicker seen in `v081_calm_compare.png` — a stated goal that the evidence suggests isn't actually fixed.
2. Give buildings real architecture: window frames, sills, cornices, brackets — not just recolored domes with circle medallions.
3. Make contact shadows soft (blurred/gradient falloff) instead of the current flat solid-black ellipses under props.
4. Soften the light itself — reduce contrast on sunbeam shafts and specular hotspots, not just the surface palette.
5. Clean the residual hot-pink HUD chrome (timer pill) to match the new cream environment palette.

SOFT: 6/10
DETAIL: 4/10
