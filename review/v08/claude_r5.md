## Review: v0.8 vs v0.7

**POLY — 8/10**
The wireframe pairs confirm real density gains: pet bodies/heads/legs now carry visibly rounder, multi-ring bevels instead of flat facets (a_poly_pets_wire.png vs b_poly_pets_wire.png), and the sofa arms/cushions/back have gone from blocky to smoothly chamfered (a_poly_sk_sofa_wire.png). Silhouettes read noticeably softer at the pet ears, tail, and sofa armrests. Minor nit: background architecture (columns, railings) still shows some hard faceting at grazing angles — fine for a secondary layer, but if budget allows, bevel the street lamp posts/columns next since they sit close to camera in several shots.

**LIGHTING — 8/10**
Warm key / cool fill separation reads clearly (compare the sofa scene: warm sunlit floor vs. cooler wall shadow), atmospheric fog gives the street real depth (distant shopfronts desaturate/lighten instead of staying flat-saturated like v0.7), and bloom is tasteful around the bedroom's spotlighted cake and window glare. Rim light is subtle but present on the pets' backs against the shop backdrop. Only soft spot: contact shadows under props (cake stand, sofa legs, pets' feet) are quite faint, so objects feel slightly like they're floating rather than grounded — a touch more AO/contact darkening would help without breaking the gentle mood.

**PALETTE_COMFORT — 7/10**
This is the big win of the patch. Street sky is now a believable blue-to-cream daytime gradient (no more lavender dome), the bedroom floor swapped its purple checker for a cream/soft-sky-blue checker, and both bedroom and living room ceilings are warm wood instead of saturated magenta — the single largest fix, since ceiling color was tinting the whole room before. Purple cast is gone from the road and ambient fill. However, two holdovers keep this from an 8+: the living-room sofa + round rug are still a large, fairly saturated bubblegum-pink mass anchoring the center of frame (reads as primary color, not accent), and the bedroom window shutters/headboard trim are pink panels large enough to compete with the new cream walls rather than sit as a small accent. Neither is purple, so the "no purple cast" goal is met, but "pink only as small accents" isn't quite there yet in these two spots.

**OVERALL — 8/10**
Strong, well-targeted pass — the purple problem is essentially solved and the lighting upgrade adds real atmosphere without going saccharine. What's left is trimming the last two oversized pink surfaces down to accent scale.

**Top fixes**
1. Living room sofa + rug: shift base fabric from bubblegum pink toward terracotta/dusty clay (or desaturate significantly), keep pink only on a cushion or two.
2. Bedroom window shutters/headboard: reduce panel size or shift to dusty rose/terracotta so it reads as trim, not a dominant surface.
3. Street scene: sidewalk curb edging and the 豆花 shop awning are still pink/lavender-leaning — nudge toward terracotta or warm cream to match the new sky treatment.
4. Add a touch more contact shadow/AO under grounded props (cake, sofa, pet feet) so the softer global lighting doesn't read as floaty.

SCORE: 8/10
