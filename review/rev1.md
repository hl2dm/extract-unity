**Score: 6.5/10**

**1. Font (GenSenRounded Bold)** — Legible and friendly, fits the toy/pastel tone well in the UI and dialogue box (阿姨家 line, shop UI). On the 豆花 sign it's oversized and the rounded bowl-terminals collide with the sign's rounded-rect border, making it feel cramped rather than crafted.

**2. Sign text vs. board** — Contrast is weak: dusty-rose text on cream card is low-chroma-on-low-chroma, borderline WCAG fail. Text also fills ~90% of the sign with almost no margin, reading as "stretched to fit" rather than designed.

**3. Street houses** — Good silhouette variety and color-blocking at a distance (r_street), but close-up (r_spawn/r_sign) shows repeated donut/ring motifs reused as the only ornament language, flat wall panels, and buildings appear to float — no visible curb, step, or shadow contact line where structure meets ground.

**Top 5 fixes:**
1. Add dark outline/drop-shadow behind sign text or shift to higher-contrast ink color (deep plum/navy) for AA-level contrast.
2. Shrink sign text ~15-20%, add consistent padding so it doesn't crowd the card edge.
3. Add grounding details: curbs, door thresholds, AO shadow strip at base of every building.
4. Vary close-range ornament set (currently all circular rings) — mix in 2-3 new trim motifs per block.
5. Reduce font boldness/size on small secondary UI labels (store item subtext) to avoid rounded letterforms clumping at small size.
