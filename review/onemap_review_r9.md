**Score: 7/10**

**Reads as real buildings painted pastel?** No — the street facades are genuinely rebuilt as rounded, bulbous toy-block forms (not recolored real architecture).

**Top 5 issues:**
1. **Dog model clashes with the kit** — it's a semi-realistic low-poly dog with normal proportions/fur shading; next to the chunky rounded buildings it reads like an asset from a different game.
2. **Scooter prop (room_2) is too detailed/realistic** — thin frame, real mirrors/wheels — breaks the soft-rounded toy silhouette rule everything else follows.
3. **Signage typography is flat real-world style** — thin uniform lettering pasted on awnings (文具, 柑仔店) looks like a printed decal rather than chunky toy/plastic lettering, undercutting the diorama read.
4. **Window/grille details sit flush as 2D stickers** — the circular 鐵窗花 motif (great cultural call) is currently flat-shaded on curved surfaces rather than given visible depth/bevel, so it reads painted-on instead of molded.
5. **Bedroom furniture proportions are "real IKEA," not toy** — desk, chair, wardrobe have believable human-scale proportions instead of the exaggerated oversized/stubby toy-furniture scale the street architecture already nails.

**Top 3 strengths:**
1. Street block massing (bulbous mushroom-cap roofs, rounded corners, chunky columns) genuinely achieves toy-town diorama silhouette.
2. Consistent soft pastel palette + cel-shaded lighting across street, interiors, and props ties the world together.
3. 鐵窗花 grille motif is tastefully integrated as a recognizable circular decorative shape rather than literal photoreal ironwork — good cultural/style balance.
