## Art Review — Pastel Toy Street Scene

**Score: 6/10** — strong color/material language, let down by HUD contrast and repeated building geometry.

**1. Font (GenSenRounded):** Fits the toy aesthetic well — rounded strokes read as friendly, not childish-ugly. Works great in the shop UI (dark text on light panels) and the dialogue box (navy on white). Fails in the top HUD: white text ("媽媽叫你回家 3:56", objective line, bottom-left status) sits directly on a pale pastel sky/ground with no outline/shadow strong enough to separate it — borderline illegible at a glance.

**2. Sign ("豆花"):** Good proportion — text fills ~70% of the board with even margins, bevel + drop shadow gives it product-label depth, and the plum-on-cream contrast is solid. Minor: stroke thickness for dense characters nearly touches the board edge; a touch more padding would help at a distance.

**3. Street houses:** Crafted at small/medium scale (railings, shutters, lamps, bollards add texture), but large-scale shapes repeat — the same pretzel/flower rosette emblem is reused as the signature facade element on nearly every building, and left/right rows read as mirrored recolors of one base. Ground contact is flat/boxy with no footing variation.

**Top 5 fixes:**
1. Add dark outline/shadow to HUD white text for contrast on sky/ground.
2. Vary signature emblem per building (not one rosette repeated).
3. Break left/right mirror symmetry — stagger heights/footprints.
4. Add visible plinth/step or shadow gradient at building bases.
5. Increase sign text padding by ~15%.
