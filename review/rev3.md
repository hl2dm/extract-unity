**7/10** — solid pastel toy aesthetic, but a few things undercut polish.

**(1) Font:** GenSenRounded reads clean at UI sizes (store panel, HUD) — bold rounded weight suits the toy tone well. On the sign ("豆花") it's fine too, though the embossed/bevel effect muddies stroke edges at close range, slightly hurting crispness for a "bold rounded" face that should look crisp and candy-like.

**(2) Sign proportion/contrast:** The 豆花 sign is the weak point — mauve-brown text on a near-white/cream board gives low contrast, and the 3D emboss shadow competes with the glyphs instead of separating them. Text also runs close to the sign's edges (col/ratio) — tight margin feels cramped at this scale.

**(3) Street houses:** Medium shot reads nicely — varied storefront colors, round motifs, canopies give rhythm. But large-scale silhouettes are nearly identical blocky rounded-rectangle buildings in a flat row; small-scale detail (window trim, shop signage variety) thins out past the first 2-3 buildings, and ground contact is weak — building bases meet the pavement with no plinth/shadow/curb transition, making them feel pasted on.

**Top 5 fixes:**
1. Increase sign text contrast (darker ink or add white outline/stroke).
2. Add visible plinth/base shadow where buildings meet ground.
3. Vary building massing/roofline silhouette, not just color.
4. Pull sign text inward from board edge (more margin).
5. Flatten/reduce bevel on sign text so font edges stay crisp.
